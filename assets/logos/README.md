# Logo sources

These marks identify the organizations and tools described in the portfolio.

- `sejong.png`: user-provided Sejong University logo, https://yoqub-ux.vercel.app/a4073d97a9c85c4819f14c7cca6c890ee79c0c25.png (resized for the web).
- `uzinfocom.svg`: official UZINFOCOM site, https://uzinfocom.uz/_next/static/media/uzinfocom-logo.8612a388.svg.
- `uzinfocom-mark.svg`: the same logo with the small tagline removed, for small sizes.
- `sejong-color.png`: official full-color Sejong University logo, https://www.sejong.ac.kr/_res/sejong/_share/img/sj-common/img-logo02-active@2x.png.
- Node.js, Figma, HTML5, JavaScript, Next.js, Vue.js, Supabase, React, Python, PHP, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, PostgreSQL, Git, GitHub, Docker, Vercel: [Devicon](https://github.com/devicons/devicon), MIT. Individual brand marks remain the property of their owners.
- Claude, OpenAI, Gemini: [LobeHub Icons](https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons), MIT. Individual brand marks remain the property of their owners.
- OWASP, Burp Suite: [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons), CC0, filled with brand colors. Individual brand marks remain the property of their owners.
- BUXORO Hotel (`buxoro-hotel.svg`): mark and wordmark from the project site, https://buxoro-brand.vercel.app/hotel.
- Firestore: [SVG Repo](https://www.svgrepo.com/svg/373598/firestore), CC0.
- `sql.svg`: custom generic database symbol.
- HELIX GROUP: custom typographic wordmark and inline vector symbol in `index.html`.
